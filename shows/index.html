How to Buy Tickets

Online

Tickets may be purchased using our online ticketing system, available 24/7. You may print your tickets at home or have them on your phone when you arrive at the theater. You should receive an email confirmation of your ticket order — if you do not, please notify the Box Office, as it is likely your order did not complete.

By Email

Send your request to the Box Office at info@fauquiertheatre.org. Please include the number of tickets, ticket type (Adult, Senior, or Student), date of performance, and a daytime phone number.

At the Door

Tickets are occasionally available at the door, as we phase out that option. The best way to be sure of having seats is to purchase tickets in advance.

Season Subscribers

Season subscribers receive discounted tickets for all regular season productions — purchase five shows and receive the sixth free. Subscriptions are available online as season packages, or contact the Box Office by email at info@fauquiertheatre.org for assistance.

Sponsor or Donor Tickets

Special sponsor or donor ticket holders should contact the Box Office by email at info@fauquiertheatre.org to make a reservation.

Ticket Policy

All sales are final. In the event that you cannot make a show that you have purchased tickets for, contact the Box Office and we will do our best to exchange your tickets for another performance.

Gift Cards

Give the gift of theater — a night out on the town or a Sunday matinee performance. It's easy to order an FCT gift card online and send it right away. Need assistance? Email info@fauquiertheatre.org.
