The Clockmaker’s Daughter

May 7–23, 2027

Get Tickets

A New Fairy Tale for Spring

A clockmaker builds a girl in his lost daughter’s image—and discovers she can think for herself. He calls her Constance and asks her to stay hidden. But her curiosity draws her into the world beyond his workshop, where wonder, romance, and fear of the unfamiliar await.

Join us May 7–23, 2027 for a musical journey into the village of Spindlewood. With book, music, and lyrics by Michael Webborn and Daniel Finn, The Clockmaker’s Daughter invites us to imagine what makes us human and what it means to belong.

“It’s a folk faerytale with an original story and music.”

— Daniel Finn, co-writer, in ThisWeek Culture

Explore the story on Michael Webborn’s website.

Meet Director Jack Tessier

Jack Tessier brings experience onstage and behind the scenes to this spring’s musical. He won Best Performer in a Play (Non-Professional) for Triune’s Larry, the Big-Time Broadway Producer in the 2023 BroadwayWorld Washington, DC Awards, an audience-voted recognition.

His imaginative work extends backstage: DC Theater Arts’ review of Triune’s Finding Neverland praised how his lighting distinguished everyday reality from Barrie’s imagination.

We can’t wait to welcome you into the world he and our company will create for The Clockmaker’s Daughter.
