The Clockmaker’s Daughter

May 7–23, 2027

Get Tickets

About the Show

A lonely clockmaker creates the daughter he dreamed of.

When she comes to life, she longs to see the wider world.

This new musical blends romance, magic, and heart.

Production

Directed by Jack Tessier

Book, music, and lyrics by Michael Webborn and Daniel Finn

This amateur performance is presented by arrangement with

Keddie Scott Associates Limited.

Performances

  • May 7, 8, 14, 15, 21, and 22 at 7:30 PM
  • May 9, 16, and 23 at 2:00 PM
