A Christmas Story

December 4–13, 2026

Get Tickets

About the Show

Ralphie Parker wants a genuine Red Ryder BB gun for Christmas.

His family, teacher, and even Santa warn him:

“You’ll shoot your eye out!”

The stage adaptation includes the beloved film’s furnace,

school bully, frozen lamppost, decoder pin, and leg lamp.

Production

Director: Leslie Anne Ross

Produced by special arrangement with

The Dramatic Publishing Company of Woodstock, Illinois.
