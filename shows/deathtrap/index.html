About the Show

Broadway playwright Sidney Bruhl needs a comeback. His recent plays have flopped, money is running low, and inspiration is elusive. Then a student sends him a thriller with all the makings of a hit.

With his wife helping him plan, Sidney proposes a collaboration, and the eager young writer accepts. But what begins as a promising partnership becomes a contest of hidden motives and startling reversals. Ira Levin’s Deathtrap blends dark humor with mounting suspense, keeping audiences guessing about who is really writing the ending.

Story summary adapted from Concord Theatricals.

Join the Mystery

Is it a comedy? Is it a thriller? The twists and turns are unexpected, surprising, and fun! Can you spot all the clues? I bet you can’t! Come join the mystery and determine what’s going on!

— Scott Olson, Director

Sidney Bruhl used to be Broadway’s master of murder. Now he’s a washed-up playwright with a string of flops and a drawer full of ideas he shouldn’t be having. Then a former student mails him a script. It’s flawless. It’s a guaranteed hit. And there’s only one copy.

Ira Levin’s Deathtrap is a thriller where everyone is smiling and no one is telling the truth. Just when you think you know who’s holding the knife, the play twists it. Then twists it again.

— Maggie Swan, Producer
