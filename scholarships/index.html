College Scholarships

Financial scholarships for local high school seniors pursuing further education in the theatre arts.

Fauquier Community Theatre invites college-bound high school seniors with demonstrated abilities in the performing arts to apply for scholarships.

FCT offers financial scholarships for local high school seniors who desire to pursue further education in the theatre arts. With the help of an FCT scholarship, young aspiring actors, actresses, musicians, and theatre technicians are aided in deferring the cost of higher education as they seek to acquire further training.

There were at least two $1,500 scholarships awarded in 2026, paid directly to the recipient's school of choice to cover tuition, books, fees, or room and board. The application deadline for 2026 scholarships was April 1, 2026 — watch this page for the next application cycle.

How Applications Are Evaluated

All applications are reviewed by a committee and scored using a rubric that includes grade point average, interest in a career in theatre, participation in Fauquier Community Theatre activities, other theater experience, short essay questions, letters of recommendation, and more.

How to Apply

  1. Complete the online scholarship application form. There is a “Save and Continue” option so you can complete the application over time.
  2. Attach two letters of recommendation from adults (excluding relatives) detailing your special qualifications for this award.
  3. Include information on any volunteer activities you have participated in at the Fauquier Community Theatre. Special consideration is given to applicants who have participated in FCT-related events.
  4. Include a copy of your high school transcript.

No late or incomplete applications will be considered. After decisions are made by the scholarship committee, chosen recipients must provide, in writing, the college's complete address as well as all information necessary to credit the student's college account.
