A Christmas Story

Performances December 4–13, 2026

Directed by Leslie Ann Ross and produced by Don Richardson, this beloved holiday comedy brings Christmas wishes, family antics, and nostalgic fun to the stage.

Follow 9-year-old Ralphie Parker on his determined Christmas quest for a genuine Red Ryder BB gun. The stage adaptation brings the beloved film’s unforgettable family, schoolyard adventures, frozen lamppost, decoder pin, leg lamp, and larger-than-life childhood fantasies to the stage.

“What I love most about this production is how it brings our community together. With adults and children sharing the stage, we get to watch generations collaborate, learn, and create something joyful.”

— Leslie Ann Ross, Director

Book early for this Christmas classic! Enjoy double-show Saturdays on December 5 and 12, with performances at 2 p.m. and 7:30 p.m.

About Us

Your community theatre

Fauquier Community Theatre was organized in 1978 by a group of committed volunteers who wanted to provide theater productions for the local community. It has been performing ever since — more than 230 productions and counting.

Today FCT stages its productions in the Vint Hill Theater on the Green, on the grounds of the old Vint Hill Army Base near Warrenton. The theatre seats approximately 200 people and has an orchestra pit and backstage facilities for small plays or large musicals.
