Step behind the scenes with Fauquier Community Theatre’s Intro to Tech Workshops! Explore the principles of sound and lighting in two practical sessions this November.

Introduction to Theatrical Sound

Saturday, November 7, 2026 · 9 a.m.–noon
Led by Sean Salas

Learn how audio, sound patterns, and microphones shape what an audience hears, then get hands-on experience with multiple soundboards.

Introduction to Theatrical Lighting

Saturday, November 14, 2026 · 9 a.m.–noon
Led by Jack Tessier

Explore lighting design fundamentals and different types of lights. See live demonstrations of light programming, rigging, and focusing.
