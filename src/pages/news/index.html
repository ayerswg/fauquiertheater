Cast and Production Team Announced for Anastasia: The Musical

June 25, 2026

Anastasia: The Musical will take place at Fauquier Community Theatre on Fridays and weekends from September 18 – October 4, 2026. Tickets ($18–$24) are on sale now at fctstage.thundertix.com.

The production team includes director Camden Gillespie, producer Maggie Swan, assistant director/dramaturg Lily VanBrocklin, music director Nick Villacorte, choreographer Abby Chaumont, stage manager Esther Wells, production stage manager Elizabeth Chaumont, tech director Stacy King, sound designer Hannah Correll, set construction manager Steve Cooper, costumer Cade Mac Aodhagáin, hair and makeup designer Ryan Kincade, and props team Linda Correll and Jessie Chen.

DC Theater Arts reviewed our world premiere musical: “'Tales of the Artisan' brings magic to Fauquier Community Theatre.” The magical new musical, with an original score by Matt Moore and Cate Murray, ran May 1–17, 2026.
