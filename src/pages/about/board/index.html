Board Meetings

Unless directed otherwise on this site, FCT Board of Director meetings are held monthly on the third Tuesday of the month at 7 pm. For details on the location of an upcoming meeting and any other information, please contact elizabeth@fctstage.org.

Each summer, FCT holds its Annual Meeting & Lofty Awards, where members receive reports from the executive committee and executive director, elect board members, and celebrate the theater year. Watch our news and the Spotlight newsletter for dates.

Staff

FCT's day-to-day operations are coordinated with the board, community partners, actors, directors, and production teams to deliver live theatre experiences — with arts education playing a role as well, especially for our youth, as they learn about acting, singing, dancing and expanding their creativity.
