Board Meetings

Unless directed otherwise on this site, FCT Board of Director meetings are held monthly on the third Tuesday of the month at 7 pm. For details on the location of an upcoming meeting and any other information, please contact info@fctstage.org.

Each summer, FCT holds its Annual Meeting & Lofty Awards, where members receive reports from the executive committee and executive director, elect board members, and celebrate the theater year. Watch our news and the Spotlight newsletter for dates.

Staff

Executive Director — Debra H. Smyers

As of February 1, 2020, Debra Smyers became the full-time executive director of Fauquier Community Theatre. She coordinates with the board, community partners, actors, directors, and production teams to deliver live theatre experiences. “Arts education plays a role also, especially for our youth, as they learn about acting, singing, dancing and expanding their creativity.”

Debra holds a Master of Arts in Arts Management from George Mason University and is an adjunct professor in the program, teaching courses in grant writing, strategic planning, and performing arts management. She has served on grant review panels for the Arts Council of Fairfax County and the Virginia Commission for the Arts, and has over 15 years of experience on the business side of the arts, having worked as an arts consultant, co-executive director, and executive director.

In Warrenton she is known as “The Arts Lady” and writes a monthly arts column for the Fauquier Times. Artistically, she has produced, directed, and performed in theatrical productions and is an actress and member of SAG-AFTRA.

Reach Debra at Debra@FCTstage.org or through the theatre message line at 540-349-8760.
