About Us

Your community theatre

Fauquier Community Theatre was organized in 1978 by a group of committed volunteers who wanted to provide theater productions for the local community. It has been performing ever since — more than 230 productions and counting.

Today FCT stages its productions in the Vint Hill Theatre, on the grounds of the old Vint Hill Army Base near Warrenton. The theatre seats approximately 200 people and has an orchestra pit and backstage facilities for small plays or large musicals.
