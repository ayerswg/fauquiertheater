About the Show

At a townhouse in Sneden’s Landing, New York,

the deputy mayor has suffered a mysterious flesh wound.

His wife is missing as their anniversary guests arrive.

Four couples scramble to agree on a story.

Confusion and miscommunication explode into classic farce.
