Sponsors

A heartfelt thank you to the businesses and organizations that partner with FCT.

As we all work together to make the community a better place, Fauquier Community Theatre greatly appreciates business sponsors and community partnerships.

Local businesses know the importance of partnering with and supporting community arts at Fauquier Community Theatre. Business sponsors have partnered with FCT year after year as they continue to find that this is a great way to give back to the community through this non-profit 501(c)(3) organization.

Interested in sponsoring the season? Please contact FCT at info@fauquiertheatre.org. Business sponsors also receive recognition in our playbill.
