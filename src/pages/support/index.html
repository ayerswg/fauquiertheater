Membership & Donations

Support the arts in Fauquier County — and enjoy some special benefits along the way.

Join or Contribute Online

Why Contribute?

First of all, you'll be supporting the arts in Fauquier County. Whether you work on our productions or simply enjoy them from the audience, your donations help make our productions possible. In addition to knowing you're helping to keep the arts alive in our community, you will receive some other special benefits as a donor.

And best yet: individuals giving at the levels below have a standing invitation to the complimentary Meet and Greet receptions on opening night of each main stage show — fun opportunities to meet the cast and crew, learn about what's happening on stage and behind the scenes, and enjoy some friendship.

All donors also receive the right to vote (over age 18) on important FCT matters at the Annual Member Meeting, and donors at the Angel level ($250) and above may have their name listed on our Angels page.

Membership Details

FCT current paid memberships are necessary to vote at FCT Annual Membership meetings. Members of and candidates for the Board of Directors are required to be current members, as are all adult employees, show directors, producers, and cast members, with the exception of orchestra musicians. Musicians and youth (age 18 and under) are encouraged to become members; youth members do not have a vote at meetings.

Cast memberships are available at the Youth Membership level of $20 for ages 17 and under, participant ($25–$49) and family participant ($50–$99) levels, and are valid for the entire fiscal year July 1 – June 30. If you and your family are already FCT lifetime Angel donors, you are exempt from paying membership fees.

If there is a financial hardship for a youth membership, please contact FCT at info@fauquiertheater.org.

How to Give

  • Online: Join and/or make a contribution through our secure ticketing system.
  • By mail: Send a check with the Angel donor form to Fauquier Community Theatre, P.O. Box 3046, Warrenton, VA 20188.
  • Questions: info@fauquiertheater.org.

FCT is a nonprofit 501(c)(3) organization. Only donations beyond benefits constitute contributions to FCT for federal income tax purposes.
