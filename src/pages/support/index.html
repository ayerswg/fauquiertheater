Why Contribute?

Your support helps turn scripts into scenery, rehearsals into performances, and a night at the theatre into a shared community experience. Membership keeps you connected to FCT, while donations help make our productions possible.

Benefits of Giving

Supporters at the levels above are invited to complimentary opening night Meet and Greet receptions. Donors at the Angel level ($250) and above may choose website recognition or remain anonymous.

Membership Details

Current paid members age 18 and older may vote at FCT Annual Membership meetings. Members of and candidates for the Board of Directors are required to be current members, as are all adult employees, show directors, producers, and cast members, with the exception of orchestra musicians. Musicians and youth (age 17 and under) are encouraged to become members; youth members do not have a vote at meetings.

Cast memberships are available at the Youth Membership level of $20 for ages 17 and under, Individual Membership ($25–$49) and Family Membership ($50–$99) levels, and are valid for the entire fiscal year July 1 – June 30. If you and your family are already FCT lifetime Angel donors, you are exempt from paying membership fees.

If a youth membership presents a financial hardship, please get in touch using the contact information below.

How to Give

  • Online: Join and/or make a contribution through our secure ticketing system.
  • By mail: Contact us for the Angel donor form and mailing instructions before sending your contribution.

FCT is a nonprofit 501(c)(3) organization. Only donations beyond benefits constitute contributions to FCT for federal income tax purposes.
