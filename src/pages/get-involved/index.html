Get Involved

There are many ways to get involved with FCT. If you like to sing, act, design sets, or play in the orchestra, there's a place for you!

Fauquier Community Theatre is all about local people sharing their talents and learning new ones. We are always looking for ushers, rehearsal pianists, stagehands, light or sound operators, seamstresses, and so much more. It's a great way to meet nice people and work together to bring something good to the community.

Directors: Propose a Show

Interested in directing one of FCT's Main Stage or youth productions? FCT is looking for directors to propose and direct shows for the 2027–2028 Main Stage season as well as summer youth productions in 2027 and 2028.

FCT's main season typically consists of 5 productions: a “classic” musical, a “contemporary” musical, a “classic” drama, comedy or mystery, and a more contemporary non-musical as well, with the fifth show selected to balance out the other four. FCT opens with musicals in September, followed by family-friendly holiday-oriented shows in December, straight drama or comedy in February and March, and another musical in May.

To propose a show, please complete the FCT Director Proposal Form. The artistic committee chairperson, Maggie Swan, is available to answer questions at elizabeth@fctstage.org.

Thank you to FCT's previous artistic committee chairs during the last decade: Don Richardson and Sheryl Solow!
