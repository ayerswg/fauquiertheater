Board Meetings

Unless directed otherwise on this site, FCT Board of Director meetings are held monthly on the third Tuesday of the month at 7 pm. For details on the location of an upcoming meeting and any other information, please contact info@fauquiertheater.org.

Each summer, FCT holds its Annual Meeting & Lofty Awards, where members receive reports from the executive committee, elect board members, and celebrate the theater year. Watch our news and the Spotlight newsletter for dates.

An All-Volunteer Organization

FCT has no permanent staff — the theatre is run entirely by volunteers, from the board to the actors, musicians, and behind-the-scenes crews. Day-to-day operations are coordinated with the board, community partners, actors, directors, and production teams to deliver live theatre experiences.

Reach FCT at info@fauquiertheater.org.

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
