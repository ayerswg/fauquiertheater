A Childhood Dream Comes True in A Christmas Story

Rehearsal photo by Mindy Ratcliff of The Little Photo Shop.

For many of us, Christmas memories come with a familiar movie playing in the background. This December, Fauquier Community Theatre brings that beloved tradition to the stage with A Christmas Story, running December 4–13, 2026.

Rehearsals are well underway as we approach the Christmas season! Our cast is bringing the Parker family’s holiday adventures to life, finding the laughter, warmth, and familiar little moments that make A Christmas Story such a cherished tradition. Mindy Ratcliff of The Little Photo Shop captured this peek into rehearsals as we get ready to welcome you to the theatre in December.

A Childhood Dream Comes True

For Lloyd Rittiner, playing The Old Man is more than a role. It’s a chance to step inside a story that has been part of his own Christmas celebrations since childhood.

A Christmas Wish and a Whole Lot of Mischief

Nine-year-old Ralphie Parker has one wish for Christmas: a genuine Red Ryder BB gun. Convincing the adults in his life is another matter, as his mother, teacher, and even Santa greet the idea with the familiar warning, “You’ll shoot your eye out!”

Philip Grecian’s stage adaptation brings Ralphie’s world to life through schoolyard scrapes, family mishaps, a frozen lamppost, and that unforgettable leg lamp. Beneath the laughter is a fond look back at childhood, when Christmas felt full of possibility and every family had its own kind of magic.

Make this holiday favorite part of your family’s December plans, and join us in cheering on Lloyd and the cast of A Christmas Story.
