Announcing the Cast for The Play That Goes Wrong!

Thank you to everyone who auditioned for The Play That Goes Wrong! There were so many talented actors and only so many roles. For those not cast, please audition for future shows. Congratulations to the following actors in their roles for The Play That Goes Wrong. Performances are on Fridays and weekends January 31, 2025 – February 16, 2025. Tickets are available for purchase now for this wonderfully zany comedy, here: https://fct.ludus.com/
