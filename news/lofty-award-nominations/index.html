June 9, 2020

Lofty Award Nominations

Here is the link to the Lofty Award Nominations for the 2019 – 2020 season. In addition to the award nominations for acting roles, this year’s list announces nominations for Technical and Overall award categories in advance as well.

You may notice that a few Technical categories do not appear in the list, however, rest assured that trophies will be awarded in all qualifying categories. So don’t miss the FCT Annual Meeting and Lofty Awards ceremony on Sunday, June 28, where all will be revealed!

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
