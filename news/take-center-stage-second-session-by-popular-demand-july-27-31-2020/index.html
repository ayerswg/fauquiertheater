July 17, 2020

Take Center Stage Second Summer Camp Session by Popular Demand – July 27 – 31, 2020

Take Center Stage! FCT Showcase Camp directed by Katy Benko-Miner for ages 8 -18. The camp takes place virtually online from July 27 to July 31, 2020 from 9:30 am – 2 pm. This one-week fun and educational camp is designed to showcase the talents of young performers. Camp will consist of singing, dancing, and acting. All registrations will be accepted and each camper will be “showcased” in songs, scenes, and roles. The cost of the Take Center Stage! FCT Showcase Theater Camp is: $200 per child and $160 for an additional child in the same family. Registration is first-come, first-served. For more information and to register for Take Center Stage! one week summer camp, click: Take Center Stage Summer Camp. If you need assistance, email debra@fctstage.org or call 540-349-8760. (For your planning purposes, performances of the Take Center Stage! camp will be July 31 at 7 pm.)

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
