December 14, 2023

Casting call! What would you do if you had all of eternity?

 

This spring, enjoy a taste of forever with the new musical, “Tuck Everlasting” at Fauquier Community Theater. All roles are currently open to ages 10 to adults in their eighties! Seeking a strong, dedicated cast of about 24. If you want to play a part in this timeless tale, find the link by clicking on the “Auditions” tab on the menu at FCT’s website:

www.FCTstage.org

.

 

Pre-registration for auditions is required. Sign-ups are open right now! Auditions will take place at the Vint Hill Theater on the Green on January 26th and 28th, 2024. Exact times are listed on the audition registration form.

 

The production of “Tuck Everlasting” is based on the award-winning novel by Natalie Babbitt. Book is by Claudia Shear and Tim Federle, music by Chris Miller, and lyrics by Nathan Tysen.

 

The musical is produced by special arrangement with Concord Theatricals. A special thank you to the community sponsor Wakefield School for their support of this musical.

 

Performances of the musical “Tuck Everlasting” will take place at the Vint Hill Theater on the Green located at 4225 Aiken Drive Warrenton, VA. Performances will take place on Fridays and weekends May 3-19. Tickets are for reserved seating and may be purchased online at

www.FCTstage.org.  

Synopsis: “Tuck Everlasting” is a profound and heartwarming story about a young girl with a longing for adventure named Winnie Foster, who runs away from home and discovers a peculiar family with the apparent secret to eternal life! When Winnie discovers this secret, she must fight to protect it from those who would do anything to never die, especially the sinister, enigmatic, and wickedly charming Man in the Yellow Suit. Winnie must learn for herself about the meaning of life, and whether living forever is all that it is claimed to be.

 

There are so many great leads and supporting characters, as well as a critical ensemble that adds so much to the dance, vocal, and emotional foundation of the show. The music is equal parts energetic and heartfelt, moving the show along the percussive ticking of a clock.

 

Want to know more about this new musical before auditioning in January? Take some moments this holiday season to read or reread the book. Listen to the soundtrack. Ponder your own mortality. And follow Fauquier Community Theater on Facebook so you don’t miss any announcements related to “Tuck Everlasting” or anything else going on at FCT. Questions? Email the director Jack Tessier at TuckEverlasting@FCTstage.org.
