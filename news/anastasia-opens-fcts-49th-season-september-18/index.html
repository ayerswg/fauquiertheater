Anastasia Opens FCT's 49th Season September 18

Fauquier Community Theatre invites audiences to journey from the twilight of Imperial Russia to the vibrant streets of 1920s Paris with the acclaimed stage musical Anastasia, opening September 18.

Inspired by the beloved animated film and acclaimed stage production, Anastasia follows a courageous young woman determined to uncover the truth about her past. Filled with unforgettable music, dazzling choreography, and breathtaking theatrical moments, the production explores identity, hope, resilience, and the enduring power of family.

As the opening production of FCT’s 49th Season, Anastasia celebrates nearly five decades of live theatre in Fauquier County while looking confidently toward the future. Produced entirely by dedicated volunteers, the production showcases the extraordinary talents of local performers, musicians, designers, technicians, and community members who continue to make live theatre accessible and inspiring.

Director Camden Gillespie believes Anastasia has a unique ability to captivate audiences of every generation.

“Anastasia is a story where history and fantasy intertwine, making it the perfect show for audiences of all ages. Our remarkably talented cast brings this timeless story to life through breathtaking music, unforgettable choreography, and heartfelt performances.”

— Camden Gillespie, Director

Lily Ayers, a fourth grader, and Lennox Chen, a fifth grader, share the role of Young Anastasia, bringing the young princess to life in alternating performances. Friends both on and off the stage, the two young performers have enjoyed learning from the cast while sharing one of the musical’s most memorable roles.

For Ayers, the music, friendships, and story have made the experience especially meaningful.

“My favorite part of rehearsals is acting, singing, and spending time with the cast. It’s really special getting to share the role with my friend Lennox, too. I hope everyone comes to see this beautiful story because I think they’ll love it as much as I do. I like that the musical is based on a true story, and it’s a little different from the movie. I can’t even pick a favorite song because I love all of the music!”

— Lily Ayers, Young Anastasia

Chen feels similarly:

“I’m so excited to perform in this show! I love sharing a role because it’s really fun to see how another actor interprets the character. This cast is amazing, and the audience is in for a treat!”

— Lennox Chen, Young Anastasia

“Opening our 49th Season with Anastasia reflects everything Fauquier Community Theatre stands for. It’s a story about discovering where you belong and finding strength through community—values that have guided FCT for nearly five decades.”

— Maggie Swan, FCT Artistic Chair

Audiences can expect stunning costumes, memorable musical numbers, and an unforgettable theatrical experience featuring favorites such as “Journey to the Past,” “Once Upon a December,” and “Paris Holds the Key (To Your Heart).” Whether discovering the story for the first time or revisiting a beloved classic, Anastasia offers an evening of spectacle and heart for all ages.

Special understudy performance: Emmy Beach will perform on Saturday, September 26, 2026.

Tickets are on sale now, and audiences are encouraged to reserve seats early for this highly anticipated opening production of FCT’s 49th Season.

About the Production

Anastasia: The Musical, created by a Tony Award-winning team featuring a book by Terrence McNally, music by Stephen Flaherty, and lyrics by Lynn Ahrens, brings the beloved story to the stage with a soaring contemporary score and unforgettable theatrical spectacle.

Book by Terrence McNally
Music by Stephen Flaherty
Lyrics by Lynn Ahrens

Inspired by the Twentieth Century Fox Motion Pictures by special arrangement with Buena Vista Theatrical

About Fauquier Community Theatre

Founded in 1978, Fauquier Community Theatre is a volunteer-driven nonprofit organization dedicated to enriching the community through exceptional live theatre, arts education, and creative opportunities for performers and audiences of all ages. Entering its 49th Season, FCT continues its tradition of producing high-quality theatrical experiences while fostering creativity, connection, and lifelong learning throughout the region.
