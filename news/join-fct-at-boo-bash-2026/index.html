Join FCT at the Trunk or Treat Boo Bash!

Fauquier Community Theatre is participating in this year’s Trunk or Treat Boo Bash! Bring your family and friends, enjoy the Halloween festivities, and come say hello to FCT.

Plan Your Visit

  • When: Sunday, October 25, 2026, from 2–6 p.m.
  • Where: Erin’s Elderberries, 4257 Aiken Drive, Warrenton, VA

Hosted by Erin’s Elderberries in collaboration with Fauquier County Parks and Recreation, the Boo Bash brings the community together for an afternoon of seasonal fun. The announced activities include trunk-or-treating, carnival games, face painting, a bounce house, laser tag, and food trucks.

We’re excited to be part of the celebration and connect with our theatre community. We hope to see you there!

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
