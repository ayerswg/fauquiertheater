FCT Annual Meeting and Lofty Awards

FCT Annual Meeting and Lofty Awards

Be our Guest! The FCT Annual Meeting and Lofty Awards will take place on Sunday, August 20 at 6:30 pm at the Vint Hill Theater on the Green located at 4225 Aiken Drive, Warrenton, VA. The Annual Meeting will include reports from the executive committee and executive director, as well as 2023 – 2024 members electing some additional board members. The Lofty Awards winners will be announced and entertainment will include the theater year in review!

To join on renew membership which is open to the public, see the Membership and Contribution tab for 2023 – 2024 on the FCT website at www.FCTstage.org.

The membership of Fauquier Community Theatre will elect three Directors to the Board for a three-year term at the Annual Meeting on August 20th. FCT seeks to have a diverse Board that represents all of its members and participants. The Board also seeks to have Directors with backgrounds that support specific Board functions, such as fundraising and community outreach, as well as someone with a financial or accounting background. For more information, contact FCT’s secretary Saralyn Aylor (SaralynA@FCTstage.org), no later than August 1st.

Lofty Award Nominations are posted on the FCT website home page: www.FCTstage.org
