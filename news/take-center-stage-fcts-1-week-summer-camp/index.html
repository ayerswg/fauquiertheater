Take Center Stage – FCT’s 1 Week Summer Camp

FCT presents its 1 week Summer Camp

Take Center Stage
Directed by Katy Benko-Miner
AGES 8 – 13
Camp Registration Fee $230

July 8 – 12, 2019
9:30 am – 2:00 pm
Performances July 12 at 7 pm and July 13 at 1 pm

If social distancing is still in place, Take Center Stage camp will be done virtually and at a discounted price to participants.

This one-week fun and educational camp is designed to showcase the talents of young performers. Camp will consist of  singing, dancing and acting. All registrations will be accepted and each camper will be “showcased” in songs, scenes and roles.

Quick Link: Click HERE to register for Take Center Stage Summer Camp!

The campers will be asked to attend a pre-camp meeting. They can choose either of the these dates for attendance: Friday, May 31 from 5-7 pm or Saturday, June 1 from 10-12 pm. Please send any questions to camp director, Katy Benko-Miner at Katybenkostudios@gmail.com. 

Director Bio:

Katy has been starring (and receiving awards) in her roles at FCT such as Martha in The Secret Garden, Winifred Banks in Mary Poppins, Patsy Cline in Always…Patsy Cline and Doris Day in Far From Home. A two-time World Champion Vocalist at the World Championships of Performing Arts in Hollywood, Katy gained notoriety as a singer in the Washington D.C. Metro Area at the age of 16, opening for acts such as Kenny Rogers, Brooks & Dunn, Brad Paisley, Toby Keith, Trace Adkins, and Alan Jackson. She has been featured on NPR, CNN International, NBC, ABC, CBS, and ESPN Sports Center. She has performed at The Patriot Center, Nissan Pavilion, The Birchmere, Jammin’ Java, and has sung the National Anthem for every professional sports team in the Washington, DC area (including the Redskins.) In 2007, Katy was invited to sing in Charleston, South Carolina at the personal request of Senator John McCain. Senator McCain then offered Katy a ride back to DC on his private plane so she could accept an invitation to be a personal guest at The White House the next morning, where she was honored to meet and sing for President Bush and First Lady Laura Bush.
