Congratulations to FCT's 2026 Lofty Award Winners and Nominees

Fauquier Community Theatre celebrated the artists and volunteers who made the 2025–2026 season shine at the annual Lofty Awards on August 16, 2026. The ceremony honored outstanding work on stage and behind the scenes across Dirty Work at the Crossroads, Calendar Girls, Bright Star, White Christmas, and Tales of the Artisan.

Congratulations to every winner and nominee, and thank you to the entire FCT community for another memorable season. Winners are listed first and marked below.

Technical Awards

Most Outstanding Set Design

Winner: Peter Marsh — Tales of the Artisan
Nominees: Doug Barylski — Dirty Work at the Crossroads; Maggie Swan — Bright Star

Most Outstanding Set Construction

Winner: Peter Marsh — Tales of the Artisan
Nominee: Steve Cooper — Bright Star

Most Outstanding Set Decoration

Winner: Maggie Swan — Bright Star
Nominees: Heather Rittiner — Calendar Girls; Stacy King — Tales of the Artisan

Most Outstanding Properties Design

Winner: Linda Correll — Tales of the Artisan
Nominees: Jay Wells — White Christmas; Pat Jannell — Calendar Girls

Most Outstanding Lighting Design

Winner: Stacy King — Tales of the Artisan
Nominees: Stacy King — Bright Star and White Christmas

Most Outstanding Sound Design

Winner: Ben Jacobeen — Tales of the Artisan
Nominee: Brian Altmiller — Bright Star

Most Outstanding Special Effects

Winner: Matt Moore — Tales of the Artisan

Most Outstanding Costume Design

Winner: Ruth Williamson — White Christmas
Nominees: Colleen Corrado — Dirty Work at the Crossroads; Heidi Santschi — Tales of the Artisan

Most Outstanding Makeup Design

Winner: Ryan Kincade — White Christmas
Nominee: Ryan Kincade — Bright Star

Most Outstanding Hair Design

Winner: Ryan Kincade — White Christmas
Nominee: Ryan Kincade — Bright Star

Performance Awards

Most Outstanding Ensemble Member in Bright Star

Winner: Margaret Reeves as Spirit

Most Outstanding Ensemble Member in White Christmas

Winner: Jacqueline Cooksey as featured dancer
Nominees: Camden Gillespie as Sheldrake’s Secretary; Nick Villacorte as dance captain; Veronica Frank as featured dancer

Jacqueline earned the highest recorded score for a featured dancer in Lofty history.

Most Outstanding Ensemble Member in Tales of the Artisan

Winner: Alyssa Luehring as Rosie
Nominee: Connor Dearinger as Fusilier Adams

Best Cameo Performer in a Musical

Winner: Tammy West as County Clerk — Bright Star
Nominee: Anthony Fuller as Dr. Norquist — Bright Star

Best Featured Actor in a Play

Winner: Lloyd Rittiner as John — Calendar Girls
Nominee: Greg Smith as Lawrence — Calendar Girls

Best Featured Actress in a Play

Winner: Kayla Beardsley as Fleurette / Little Nell puppet — Dirty Work at the Crossroads
Nominee: Krista Poole as Elaine — Calendar Girls

Best Featured Actor in a Musical

Winner: Scott Pierce as Ezekial Foster — White Christmas
Nominees: Garrett Tucker as Mike Nulty — White Christmas; Hunter Elliott as Finn — Tales of the Artisan; Weston Weber as Darryl Ames — Bright Star

Best Featured Actress in a Musical

Winner: Kayce Schaffer as Rhoda — White Christmas
Nominees: Mason Campbell as Lucy Grant — Bright Star; Melissa Pieja as Rita — White Christmas; Pam Gordet as Mama Murphy — Bright Star

Best Supporting Actress in a Play

Winner: Leslie Anne Ross as Ruth — Calendar Girls
Nominees: Ellen Tolson as Widow Lovelace / Mrs. Upson Asterbilt — Dirty Work at the Crossroads; Tammy Barboza as Celia — Calendar Girls; Tina Mullins as Jessie — Calendar Girls

Best Supporting Actor in a Musical

Winner: W. Gregory Smith as Mayor Josiah Dobbs — Bright Star
Nominees: Chris Winkler as General Hank Waverly — White Christmas; Ryan L. Schaffer as Ralph Sheldrake — White Christmas; W. Gregory Smith as Captain Fallow — Tales of the Artisan

Best Supporting Actress in a Musical

Winner: Lennox Chen as Susan Waverly — White Christmas
Nominees: Avery Heisey as Siobhan — Tales of the Artisan; Camden Gillespie as Margo Crawford — Bright Star; Dawn D Gaynor as Martha Watson — White Christmas

Best Lead Actor in a Play

Winner: Anthony Fuller as Munro Murgatroyd — Dirty Work at the Crossroads

Best Lead Actress in a Play

Winner: Eileen Marshall as Annie — Calendar Girls
Nominees: Cheryl Bolt as Chris — Calendar Girls; Elizabeth Steimel as Ida Rhinegold — Dirty Work at the Crossroads; Rachel Scott as Nellie Lovelace — Dirty Work at the Crossroads

Best Lead Actor in a Musical

Nominees: Noah Sarinana as Jimmy Ray Dobbs — Bright Star; Noah Sarinana as Phil Davis — White Christmas

A first in Lofty history: a dead-heat tie for the same performer in two different roles.
