Girl Scouts Discover Backstage Magic at Anastasia

The curtain may have fallen on Anastasia, but the memories keep shining. During our September 18–October 4, 2026 production, Girl Scouts got a closer look at the imagination and teamwork behind the magic.

A Look Behind the Curtain

The Girl Scouts enjoyed a backstage tour, met some of the cast, and saw the exquisite props designed by Linda Correll up close. It was a chance to discover the careful details that help bring a story to life, beyond what an audience sees from its seats.

From meeting performers to exploring the world behind the curtain, the visit added another special memory to a production we were so proud to share. Thank you to the Girl Scouts, their accompanying adults, and our theatre team for making this backstage experience possible!

Linda Correll’s Props Up Close

These are some of the exquisite props made by Linda Correll for our past production of Anastasia. From an ornate golden egg shaped music box to a ribbon-trimmed doll, each detail helped create the world of the show—and gave our Girl Scout visitors something special to discover backstage.

Another Backstage Adventure This Spring

We’re looking forward to welcoming Girl Scouts for another free post-show Girl Scout Backstage Experience following a May 2027 performance of The Clockmaker’s Daughter.

Tickets to this free after-show event will be available in spring 2027. Check back for the date and registration details. The musical runs May 7–23, 2027; tickets for the show are separate from the free backstage event.
