Community Theatre Thrives! Entertainment and Telethon!

July 17 &18, 2020: Fourteen all-volunteer theatres from DC, Maryland and Virginia are coming together for a two-day fundraising telethon, to ensure that local performing arts continue to thrive in the Washington DC region for years to come. This one-time event is being coordinated by the Reston Community Players, one of the region’s most established all-volunteer theatre companies.
Here is the schedule of participating organizations:
FRIDAY JULY 17
6:30pm Welcome
7-8pm Reston Community Players
8-9pm City of Fairfax Theatre Company
9-10pm Laurel Mill Playhouse
10-11pm The Victorian Lyric Opera Company 10:00 AM
SATURDAY JULY 18
10-11am Reston Community Players
11-12n The Colonial Players of Annapolis
12-1pm Silver Spring Stage
1-2pm The Arlington Players
2-3pm Rockville Little Theatre
3-4pm Fauquier Community Theatre
4-5pm Sterling Playmakers
5-6pm Prince William Little Theatre
6-7pm The Hard Bargain Players
7-8pm Kensington Arts Theatre
8-9pm Annapolis Summer Garden Theatre

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
