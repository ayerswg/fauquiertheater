FCT Shares 2025–2026 Annual Report

Fauquier Community Theatre is pleased to share our 2025–2026 Annual Report at the Annual Meeting this evening, highlighting a year of artistic programming, community engagement, youth education, organizational investment, and responsible financial stewardship.

This year’s work was made possible through the combined support of our volunteers, audiences, members, individual donors, community partners, and grant funders. Their investment helps FCT provide accessible live theatre and educational opportunities while strengthening the organization for the future.

The report provides an overview of our programming, financial position, community impact, and the continued investments we are making in our facilities, operations, and long-term sustainability.

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
