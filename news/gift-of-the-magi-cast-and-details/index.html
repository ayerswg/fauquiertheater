Gift of the Magi – The Musical: Cast and Details!

Announcing the cast for this holiday classic directed by Diane King! The name of the role is listed first, followed by the name of the actor.
Della Young – Gabrielle Buonocore Jim Young – Jack Tessier Maddy – Betsy Hansen William – Martin Kelly Betsy – Carina Stinson Nestor – Mark Tessier Annabelle – Rose Tessier Hunnicutt – Jamey Pellegrini Porter/Beggar Father – Preston Mulford Officer O’Reilly – Stub Estey Officer Brown – Jiles Burnham Santa/Henry – Santa Alice/Beggar Mother – Isabella McDonald Emily – Evelyn Lehmann.

Synopsis: It’s Christmas in New York in 1905. Imagine the excitement of a busy street, bustling with shoppers and carolers singing joys of the season. Della, a stunning young woman, strolls along, looking in store windows, a bit saddened that she only has $1.87 to spend on a gift for her husband, Jim. Down the street, Jim does likewise; wistfully knowing that the items in the stores that he would love to buy for Della are beyond his means. The audience will enjoy a beautiful musical adaptation of O. Henry’s, “The Gift of the Magi.”

Performances of Gift of the Magi – The Musical will be presented on Fridays and weekends between November 26 and December 12, 2021 at the Vint Hill Theatre on the Green. The show is sponsored by NOVEC. Tickets here: www.FCTstage.org.
