“Safe and Warm” and Enchanting: YAM Productions Celebrates Anastasia

Thank you for taking the journey with us! To everyone who attended Anastasia: The Musical, shared in the laughter, and filled our theatre with applause: you helped make this production something truly special.

As we look back on our September 18–October 4 run, we are grateful to Brianna Lau of YAM Productions for a thoughtful review that celebrates both the performances audiences saw and the artistry behind them.

A Production Worth Celebrating

YAM’s review recognizes the heart and humor of our cast: Jennifer Ayers’ soaring Journey to the Past, the tenderness of her scenes with Noah Sariñana, Ryan Lucius Schaffer’s comic warmth as Vlad, and Rachel Donders’ vibrant performance as Lily. It also celebrates Hilary Pierce’s moving portrayal of the Dowager Empress and Spencer N. Pilcher’s powerful vocals as Gleb.

The ballet was a “rare treat in community theatre.”

— YAM Productions

The ballet choreography was by Abby Chaumont. Thank you, Abby, for bringing this beautiful moment to life!

The review highlights the ensemble’s contribution, the emotion of Stay, I Pray You, and Camden Gillespie’s direction. We are proud to see so many parts of this shared effort recognized.

Read the Full YAM Productions Review

Behind Every Moment of Magic

Special recognition goes to the details that helped tell the story: Linda Correll’s music box, singled out as the “hero prop,” Hannah Correll’s clean sound, and the costumes whose layers and textures helped transport audiences from St. Petersburg to Paris.

Those moments represent countless hours of care. To our cast, musicians, director, choreographer, production team, stage managers, designers, builders, costumers, props team, lighting and sound crews, backstage volunteers, and front-of-house helpers: thank you. Every rehearsal, stitch, brushstroke, cue, and welcome at the door helped bring this acclaimed production to our community.

You Made the Journey Special

Live theatre is something we create together. Whether you bought a ticket, brought a friend, volunteered your time, or cheered us on, you were part of Anastasia. Thank you for supporting community theatre and giving our 49th season such a memorable beginning.
