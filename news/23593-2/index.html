July 13, 2024

Seeking Directors for Plays and Musicals!

SEEKING DIRECTORS! Fauquier Community Theatre (FCT) is looking for Directors to propose and direct shows for some of our 2025 – 2026 and 2026 – 2027 Main Stage productions. We are also seeking directors for our Youth Shows in June of 2025 and June of 2026. FCT’s Main season typically consists of 5 productions: a “classic” musical, a “contemporary” musical, a “classic” drama, comedy or mystery, and a more contemporary non-musical as well, with the fifth show selected to balance out the other four. We open with musicals in September, followed by family-friendly, holiday oriented shows in December, straight drama or comedy in February and March, and another musical in May. If you would like to direct a show at FCT, please complete the FCT DIRECTOR PROPOSAL FORM The artistic chairpersons, Don Richardson and Maggie Swan, are available to answer questions via email at ArtisticChair@FCTstage.org.

FCT is also seeking proposals for our Youth Shows, for June 2025 and June 2026. Our Youth Shows are traditionally musicals but may be non-musicals as well. All cast for youth shows are 18 and under. Please share your ideas by completing the FCT DIRECTOR PROPOSAL FORM.  The Artistic Chairpersons are available to answer questions via email at ArtisticChair@FCTstage.org.
