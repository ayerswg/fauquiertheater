Raise Your Voice at FCT's Karaoke After Party

Raise your voice for a great cause. After FCT’s Annual Meeting and Lofty Awards, the stage belongs to you.

Join Fauquier Community Theatre for an unforgettable Karaoke After Party, where Broadway favorites, guilty pleasures, power ballads, and crowd-pleasing classics take center stage.

Whether you’re singing or cheering from the audience, every performance helps support improvements to FCT’s dressing room facilities.

Take the Stage

Limited solo and group performance spots are available. The winner takes home the prize!

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
