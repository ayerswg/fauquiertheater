FCT Voted Best Theatre by Fauquier Now

We’re honored to announce that Fauquier Community Theatre has been voted Best Theatre by Fauquier Now!

This recognition belongs to our incredible volunteers, performers, directors, designers, musicians, sponsors, donors, and audiences. Thank you for supporting local theatre!

Whether you’re a longtime patron or planning your first visit, we can’t wait to welcome you to an award-winning season of live theatre.

Thank you, Fauquier Now, for this incredible honor.

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
