Give Local Piedmont Early Giving Begins April 25!

Contributions may be made now! Bonus: As a thank you from FCT, for every $25 contribution made to FCT via Give Local Piedmont, the donor will be entered in an FCT drawing to win 2 season tickets for two people to see 5 shows each in the 2023 – 2024 season! The winner will be notified by FCT on May 10. Become a cheerful giver of supporting the Performing Arts at FCT via Give Local Piedmont here: givelocalpiedmont.org/donate/FauquierCommunityTheatre/ Founded in 1978 Fauquier Community has been providing plays and musicals, as well as youth programs, and arts education for all ages for almost half a century!

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
