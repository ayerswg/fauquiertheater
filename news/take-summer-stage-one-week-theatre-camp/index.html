March 5, 2020

Take Summer Stage! One Week Theatre Camp

Take Center Stage! FCT Showcase Camp directed by Katy Benko-Miner for ages 8 -13. The camp takes place from July 6 to July 10, 2020 from 9:30 am – 2 pm at the Vint Hill Theatre on the Green located at 4225 Aiken Drive, Warrenton, Virginia. This one-week fun and educational camp is designed to showcase the talents of young performers. Camp will consist of singing, dancing, and acting. All registrations will be accepted and each camper will be “showcased” in songs, scenes, and roles. The cost of the Take Center Stage! FCT Showcase Theater Camp is: $230 per child and $180 for an additional child in the same family. Registration is first-come, first-served, and there are a limited amount of spaces so please submit promptly. For more information and to register for Take Center Stage! one week summer camp, click: Take Center Stage Summer Camp. If you need assistance, email debra@fctstage.org or call 540-349-8760. (For your planning purposes, performances of the Take Center Stage! camp will be July 10 at 7 pm and July 11 at 1 pm.)

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
