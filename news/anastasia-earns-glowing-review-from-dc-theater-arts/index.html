Anastasia Receives Glowing Reviews from DC Theater Arts and DMV Theater

The praise is in! Anastasia: The Musical is earning glowing reviews from DC Theater Arts and DMV Theater, celebrating the performers and creative team behind the opening production of Fauquier Community Theatre’s 49th Season.

Pictured above: Ryan Lucius Schaffer as Vlad, Jennifer Ayers as Anya, and Noah Sariñana as Dimitry. Photo by The Little Photo Shop.

Some performances are at risk of selling out! Reserve your seats now to catch Anastasia before the final curtain on October 4.

Reserve Your Seats Now

DC Theater Arts: Spotlight on the Cast

Jeffrey Walker’s September 24 review in DC Theater Arts celebrates the performers. Here are a few brief highlights:

  • Jennifer Ayers — Anya: Walker says she “commands the stage” and praises the sincerity she brings to Anya.
  • Noah Sariñana — Dimitry: His “charisma” shines in the role and in his scenes with Ayers.
  • Ryan Lucius Schaffer — Vlad: Walker singles out his “comic panache and verve”.
  • Rachel Donders — Countess Lily: She arrives with a “voice to match” her bold entrance, pairing with Schaffer for a standout duet.
  • Spencer N. Pilcher — Gleb: Walker praises his “expansive and operatic voice”.
  • Hilary Pierce — Dowager Empress: Her portrayal earns praise as a “touching performance”.

Walker also recognizes the ensemble’s versatility and notes Lily Ayers and Lennox Chen sharing the young roles.

The review also applauds the direction, choreography, music, lighting, sound, and costumes that complete the production.

Read the Full Review at DC Theater Arts

DMV Theater: Four Stars

★★★★ — Four stars from DMV Theater

In its Instagram review, @dmvtheater celebrates the production’s direction, ensemble, and leading trio:

“With strong direction, a well-coordinated ensemble, and a masterful trio front and center, this is a production that gets it right where it counts.”

— DMV Theater

The review gives special recognition to:

  • Jennifer Ayers — Anya: “Jennifer Ayers is an enchanting Anya, bringing optimism and resilience to a character who has faced hardship throughout her life.”
  • Noah Sariñana — Dimitry: “Noah Sariñana’s Dmitry is streetwise and jaded but full of youthful energy, with a flicker of hope that Anya brings to the surface.”
  • Ryan Lucius Schaffer — Vlad: “Ryan Lucius Schaffer is an absolute scene-stealer as Vlad.” The review also emphasizes Vlad’s role in connecting Anya and Dmitry and driving the story forward.
  • Spencer N. Pilcher — Gleb: DMV Theater praises his “masterful performance as the antagonist Gleb, with captivating vocals and a commanding presence that slowly crumbles over the course of the show.”

Thank You to Our Reviewers

Our heartfelt thanks to Jeffrey Walker and DC Theater Arts (@dctheaterarts), and to DMV Theater (@dmvtheater), for attending Anastasia and sharing such thoughtful, enthusiastic reviews. We appreciate your recognition of our performers and creative team, and your support of community theatre.

Thank you, too, to our cast, crew, volunteers, and audiences for making this production possible!
