September 9, 2024

Cast Announced for “Scrooge! The Musical”

So many talented people auditioned for Scrooge! The Musical. Congratulations to the cast selected.

Nine performances of

Scrooge! The Musical

will take place between December 6 and December 15, 2024. Thursday, Friday and Saturday evening performances are at 7:30 pm. Saturday and Sunday matinees are at 2 pm. All performances will take place at the Vint Hill Theater on the Green located at 4225 Aiken Drive, Warrenton, VA. All tickets are for reserved seating and available online now at

https://fctstage.thundertix.com/

. Choose your favorite seating options today!
