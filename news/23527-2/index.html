March 30, 2024

Auditions Continue for Les Miserables School Edition

NEXT AUDITIONS – SATURDAY, APRIL 6, 2024 in 15 minutes intervals from 10 am to 1 pm.

FCT Summer Musical Theatre Intensive presents Les Miserables School Edition (2 hour version) directed by Jeff Walker with music director Doray Walker. This two-week program is geared for older youth for a Summer Musical Theatre Intensive of two weeks: June 17 – 21 and June 24 – 28 from 9 am to 3 pm, with a total of 4 performances June 28 – 30.  Ages: Rising middle school students – graduating high school seniors, although no older than 18. The FCT Summer Musical Theatre Intensive program is by auditions that will take place in March 2024 in Warrenton, Virginia. For more information and to register for an audition, click on Summer Youth Programs.

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
