Cast List Announced for White Christmas!

Thank you to all those who auditioned for White Christmas.

The Musical Irving Berlin’s White Christmas: Veterans Bob Wallace and Phil Davis have a successful song-and-dance act after World War II. With romance in mind, the two follow a duo of beautiful singing sisters en route to their Christmas show at a Vermont lodge, which just happens to be owned by Bob and Phil’s former army commander. With a dazzling score featuring well-known standards including “Blue Skies,” “I Love A Piano,” “How Deep Is the Ocean” and the perennial title song, White Christmas is an uplifting, wholesome musical that will delight audiences of all ages. Music and Lyrics by Irving Berlin. Book by David Ives and Paul Blake. Fauquier Community Theatre’s production of Irving Berlin’s White Christmas is directed by Esther Wells.

Performances for the holiday classic musical “White Christmas” will take place at Fauquier Community Theatre December 5 – 14, 2025. All tickets are for reserved seating. Tickets ($18 – $24) are available to purchase here: https://fct.ludus.com/
