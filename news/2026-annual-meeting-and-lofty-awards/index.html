July 16, 2026

2026 Annual Meeting & Lofty Awards

The Lofty Award winners will be honored at the Annual Membership Meeting on Sunday, August 16, 2026. The event begins at 6 pm at the Vint Hill Theater on the Green, 4225 Aiken Drive, Warrenton, Virginia.

Members from 2025–2026 and 2026–2027 receive tickets to attend, with the option to bring guests, as part of their membership benefits. Not a member? Adult memberships begin at just $25. Come join in the excitement!

The evening will include Board of Directors elections, board reports, the Lofty Awards, and entertainment.
