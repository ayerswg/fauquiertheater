Congratulations to the Cast of A Christmas Story!

Ralphie has his Christmas wish list, and we have our cast! This talented group of adults and young performers is bringing A Christmas Story to life with all the laughter, heart, and holiday mischief that make it a Christmas favorite.

Directed by Leslie Ann Ross and produced by Don Richardson, A Christmas Story runs December 4–13, 2026 at the Vint Hill Theatre on the Green.

Make your holiday plans early! Book your tickets before this Christmas classic sells out. We have double-show days on Saturday, December 5, and Saturday, December 12, with performances at 2 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. on both Saturdays.

A Christmas Wish, a Leg Lamp, and a Whole Lot of Heart

For Leslie Ann Ross, the magic goes beyond Ralphie’s Christmas wish. She is excited to bring generations together onstage, pairing the nostalgia of the 1940s setting with the energy of today’s performers. As she shared in our audition announcement:

“What I love most about this production is how it brings our community together. With adults and children sharing the stage, we get to watch generations collaborate, learn, and create something joyful.”

— Leslie Ann Ross, Director

Follow 9-year-old Ralphie Parker as he campaigns for the Christmas gift of his dreams: a Red Ryder BB gun. From the leg lamp to the frozen lamppost and the warning “You’ll shoot your eye out!”, this stage adaptation brings the beloved film’s memorable moments to life.
