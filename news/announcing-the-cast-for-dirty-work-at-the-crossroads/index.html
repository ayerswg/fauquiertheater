Announcing the cast for Dirty Work at the Crossroads

Dirty Work at the Crossroads is a comedic melodrama that tells in laughable style the tear jerking story of Nellie Lovelace, a country girl. Munro Murgatroyd is the classic villain in the play who is intent on selling some land underhandedly to the railroad. Enjoy some laughter and find out what happens at the crossroads! The play is directed by Marty Kelly, written by Bill Johnson, and produced by special arrangement with Concord Theatricals on behalf of Samuel French, Inc.

CAST LIST FOR DIRTY WORK AT THE CROSSROADS

Nellie Lovelace: Rachel Scott
Adam Oakhart: Val Kuperman
Munro Murgatroyd: Anthony Fuller
Ida Rhinegold: Elizabeth Steimel
Mookie Maguggins: Guy Callahan
The Widow Lovelace: Ellen Woodstock
