September 20, 2020

Auditions for “Its a-Wonderful Life-A Live Radio-Play” Sept 24 and 26, 2020

AUDITIONS! Fauquier Community Theatre will be presenting “It’s a Wonderful Life – A Live Radio Play” written by Joe Landry and with permission from Playscripts, Inc. Auditions will take place online via Zoom on Thursday, September 24, 2020 between 7:00 pm – 10:00 pm and Saturday, September 26, 2020 between 9:00 am – Noon. Click on the link below for the FCT Audition Form to see more information and to schedule your audition: https://bit.ly/33LJJ8T

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
