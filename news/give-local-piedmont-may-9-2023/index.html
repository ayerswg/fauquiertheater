Give Local Piedmont May 9, 2023

Founded in 1978 Fauquier Community has been providing plays and musicals, as well as youth programs, and arts education for all ages for almost half a century!

 

Bonus! For every $25 contribution made to FCT via Give Local Piedmont, the donor will be entered in an FCT drawing to win 2 season tickets for two people to see 5 shows each in the 2023 – 2024 season! The winner will be notified by FCT on May 10.

 

Become a cheerful giver of supporting the Performing Arts at FCT via Give Local Piedmont here:

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
