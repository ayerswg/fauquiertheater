April 22, 2022

Fauquier Community Theatre presents “Side by Side by Sondheim” April 29 – May 15

Enjoy these Broadway favorites that include songs from “Company”, “Follies”, “West Side Story”, “Gypsy”, “A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to the Forum”, “A Little Night Music”, and much more! For the Mother’s Day performance, we will treat moms to complimentary beverages. With standing ovations and much applause throughout, as a reviewer says, “The musical includes a cast of three principal actors and six featured actors who entertain, charm, and dazzle.” Reservations for tickets here: www.FCTstage.org

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
