Auditions for A Christmas Story

Fauquier Community Theatre is now casting A Christmas Story, the beloved holiday comedy performing December 4–13, 2026.

Directed by Leslie Ann Ross and produced by Don Richardson, the production follows 9-year-old Ralphie Parker as he sets out to get a genuine Red Ryder BB gun for Christmas. His mother, teacher, and even Santa give him the same warning: “You’ll shoot your eye out!”

The stage adaptation includes all the favorite moments from the motion picture: the family’s temperamental furnace, school bully Scut Farkas, the frozen lamppost, the Little Orphan Annie decoder pin, the leg lamp, Ralphie’s fantasy scenarios, and more.

“There’s a reason A Christmas Story has become woven into the fabric of American holiday tradition. Beneath the laughter, the leg lamp, and the unforgettable warnings about shooting one’s eye out, the story captures something timeless: the way childhood magnifies everything—hopes, fears, triumphs, and the magic of wanting one perfect gift more than anything in the world.

This play steps into Ralphie’s imagination, where everyday moments explode into heroic fantasies and minor mishaps feel like epic disasters. It celebrates the beautifully imperfect family at the heart of the story. The Old Man’s bluster, Mother’s quiet strength, and Ralphie’s wide-eyed determination remind us that the holidays are rarely perfect, but they are always memorable.

What I love most about this production is how it brings our community together. With adults and children sharing the stage, we get to watch generations collaborate, learn, and create something joyful. The nostalgia of the 1940s setting blends with the energy of today’s performers, creating a bridge between past and present.”

— Leslie Ann Ross, Director

All auditions are open, and newcomers are welcome. Complete audition details and the application are available online.
