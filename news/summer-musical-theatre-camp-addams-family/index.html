December 28, 2019

Summer Musical Theatre Camp – Addams Family

Fauquier Community Theatre presents the two-week musical theatre summer camp “The Addams Family Young@Part” for ages 11-18. The camp runs from July 20 – July 31, 2020: Monday through Friday from 9:30 am – 3:30 pm. Performances will take place on July 31 and August 1. The director is Elizabeth Nefferdorf who is a talented actress and director. For more information and to register, please click to complete the Summer Camp Form. After you choose the submit button at the end of the form, you will be taken to the payment page. The camp fee is $335 with a $50 discount for an additional family member. Registration is first come, first served, and there are a limited amount of spaces so please submit promptly. You will receive an email confirming the receipt of your registration. All campers will receive a camp t-shirt. Every student whose registration is accepted will have a part in the production. Please make credit card/debit card payment in full at time of submitting form. If you need assistance, contact AddamsFamily@FCTstage.org.
