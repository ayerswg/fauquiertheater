October 1, 2020

Congratulations to over 40 actors cast for “It’s a Wonderful Life: A Live Radio Play” by Joe Landry

Fauquier Community Theatre Audition Recap and Casting Results:


Auditions were held virtually on September 24 and 26, 2020 and 70


people signed up. Congratulations to over 40 actors who obtained roles in


It’s a Wonderful Life: A Live Radio Play

by Joe Landry. The play is directed by Fauquier Community Theatre’s Betsy Hansen with Sonia Bronder as


the Assistant Director, Lighting, and Tech Designer.


The beloved American holiday classic will be brought to life as a live


1940s radio broadcast and streamed virtually. With the help of an


ensemble that brings a few dozen characters to the virtual screen , the


story of idealistic George Bailey unfolds as he considers what’s


meaningful in life on that fateful Christmas Eve in 1946.
