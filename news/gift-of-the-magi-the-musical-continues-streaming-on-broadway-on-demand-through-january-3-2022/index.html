December 28, 2021

Gift of the Magi – The Musical Continues – Streaming on Broadway on Demand through January 3, 2022.

Fauquier Community Theatre’s Gift of the Magi – The Musical continues as a stream online! After 9 highly successful shows live on stage in Warrenton, Virginia the show is now online for those who missed it or want to see it again. The film version is beautiful with close ups of the cast! Click HERE to go directly to Broadway on Demand to view information and to see the show online from December 28, 2021 through January 3, 2022. View the musical by one of the choices on Broadway on Demand of making a donation to the theater in the range of $12 – $25. Once on the Broadway on Demand site, click on “Play” to get started!

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
