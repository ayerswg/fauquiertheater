August 6, 2021

Auditions for Gift of the Magi – The Musical

Audition Dates: Fauquier Community Theatre is delighted to have Diane King directing Gift of the Magi – The Musical. Most roles are for adults although there are some children’s roles also. Auditions for ages 7 and up are on Saturday, September 18, 2021 from 9 am to 11 am or 1 pm to 3 pm at Hope Christian Fellowship located at 4173 Bludau Dr, Warrenton, VA 20187. (Callbacks will be by invitation only on Monday, September 20 from 7 pm to 9 pm. Not all roles will need callbacks, so you may still be cast even if you do not get a callback.)

Plot Synopsis: It’s Christmas in New York in 1905. Imagine the excitement of a busy street, bustling with shoppers and carolers singing joys of the season. Della, a stunning young woman, strolls along, looking in store windows, a bit saddened that she only has $1.87 to spend on a gift for her husband, Jim. Down the street, Jim does likewise; wistfully knowing that the items in the stores that he would love to buy for Della are beyond his means. Yes, you have entered a beautiful musical adaptation of O. Henry’s, The Gift of the Magi, which will thrill and inspire cast members and audiences.

For more information regarding the character breakdown and to sign up for an audition, click on the Audition Form
