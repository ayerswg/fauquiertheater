Auditions for the melodrama play, “Dirty Work at the Crossroads” October 15 and 17, 2025

Casting Call October, 2025! Fauquier Community Theatre will be holding auditions for the classic melodrama play Dirty Work at the Crossroads! Auditions are open to the public and will take place on Wednesday October 15, 2025 from 7 pm to 10:00 pm and Friday October 17, 2025 from 7 pm to 10:00 pm.  Pre-registration is required and more information can be found on the Auditions menu tab at the FCT website: www.FCTstage.org. Auditions will take place at the Vint Hill Theater on the Green, 4225 Aiken Drive, Warrenton, VA. For more information, and to register for an audition, click on: Audition Form for Dirty Work at the Crossroads.

Synopsis! Dirty Work at the Crossroads is a comedic melodrama that tells in laughable style the tear jerking story of Nellie Lovelace, a country girl. Munro Murgatroyd is the classic villian in the play who is intent on selling some land underhandedly to the railroad. He has a wife Ida Rhinegold, belle of the New Haven Music Halls, but that does not prevent him from pursuing Nellie. Nor does it prevent him from driving Adam Oakhart, the blacksmith’s son to vices, or from blackmailing rich Mrs. Asterbilt, or from bewitching her daughter, Leonie. There are a number of places in the plot where old time songs are introduced.

See the Play in January and February, 2026! Enjoy some laughter and find out what happens at the crossroads! The play is directed by Marty Kelly, written by Bill Johnson, and produced by special arrangement with Concord Theatricals on behalf of Samuel French, Inc. The play takes place on Fridays and weekends from January 23, 2026 – February 8, 2026. Friday and Saturday performances are at 7:30 pm and Sunday matinees are at 2 pm. All performances will take place at the Vint Hill Theater on the Green, 4225 Aiken Drive, Warrenton, VA.
