Holidays for 2025

Holidays for 2025: FCT follows the Federal and Fauquier County Holiday Schedules. That is unless of course dates are specifically listed by FCT for auditions, rehearsals, performances, or summer camp. Otherwise FCT is closed for the following Federal Holidays. In addition, details regarding Fauquier County Holidays may be found on the Fauquier County website HERE

FEDERAL HOLIDAY CLOSURES:

  • New Year’s Day
  • Martin Luther King Day
  • George Washington Birthday
  • Memorial Day
  • Juneteenth,
  • Independence Day
  • Labor Day
  • Columbus Day
  • Election Day
  • Veterans Day
  • Thanksgiving Day
  • Christmas Day

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
