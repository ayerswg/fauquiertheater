December 29, 2019

Jesus Christ Superstar Auditions

“Jesus Christ Superstar” will be directed by Matt Moore and produced by Mary Beth Balint. Auditions will be held at the John Barton Payne building located at 2 Courthouse Square, Warrenton, Virginia (in Warrenton’s Old Town historic district.) Audition dates are: Friday, February 21, 2020 at 7 pm and Saturday, February 22 at 11 am and 2 pm. Auditions are open to the public and all levels of experience are welcome. For more information and to sign up for an audition, click Audition Form.

Synopsis: “Jesus Christ Superstar” is a rock opera based on the last week of Jesus’ life. Lyrics by Tim Rice and music by Andrew Lloyd Webber. The first musical by Andrew Lloyd Webber and Tim Rice to be produced for the professional stage, Jesus Christ Superstar has wowed audiences for over 40 years. A timeless work, the rock opera is set against the backdrop of an extraordinary and universally-known series of events but seen, unusually, through the eyes of Judas Iscariot. Loosely based on the Gospels of Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John, Superstar follows the last week of Jesus Christ’s life. The story, told entirely through song, explores the personal relationships and struggles between Jesus, Judas, Mary Magdalene, his disciples, his followers, and the Roman Empire. The iconic 1970s rock score contains such well-known numbers as “Superstar,” “I Don’t Know How to Love Him” and “Gethsemane.”
