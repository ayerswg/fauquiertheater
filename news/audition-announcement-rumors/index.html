Audition Announcement: Rumors

Have you heard? Audition registration is now open for Fauquier Community Theatre’s production of Rumors by Neil Simon! When four couples arrive for a dinner party only to find the host wounded and his wife missing, they plunge into a hysterical, fast-paced spiral of cover-ups and escalating chaos.

Audition Dates

  • Friday, October 16, 2026 at 6:30 PM
  • Saturday, October 17, 2026 at 12:30 PM

Auditions are open to actors 18 and older, and newcomers are welcome! For more information and to sign up for an audition slot, visit the registration link below.

Register for Auditions

Directed by Marty Kelly
Produced by Sam Wong
