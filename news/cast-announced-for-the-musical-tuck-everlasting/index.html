February 4, 2024

Cast Announced for the musical Tuck Everlasting!

From the Director and Production Team:

Everyone, We were absolutely amazed by your performances at callbacks! It was a long process, but each and every one of you showed strength and endurance throughout. Please know that choosing the cast was difficult due to all the talent. However, after several hours of deliberation, we were able to complete the Cast List for Tuck Everlasting.

If you don’t see your name on the list, we hope you are still proud of yourself for making it to callbacks and we wish you the very best in all your future theatre endeavors. It was a pleasure to watch you perform and share your talents with us! As always, feel free to reach out if you would like any feedback on your performance. This time with just 28 roles and a record number auditioning for a main stage show, we regret that we could not cast everyone. We hope to see you at future auditions! For youth auditioning, please see our summer youth programs that includes Les Miserables Student Edition.
