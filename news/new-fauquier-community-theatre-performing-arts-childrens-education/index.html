New! Fauquier Community Theatre Performing Arts Children’s Education!

New! Fauquier Community Theatre Performing Arts Children’s Education! Classes taught by Katy Benko-Miner and her team will take place on Mondays beginning on September 28 through November 16 for ages 8-16 and will be run virtually online. Classes will consist of acting, singing, and choreography options. The new FCT PACE program is launched with assistance by the Patricia and Nicolaas Kortlandt Community Fund at Northern Piedmont Community Foundation. For more information, click on the link to the registration form: https://bit.ly/32LDRNr

Fauquier Community Theatre is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.

FCT's programs and projects are supported in part by the Virginia Commission for the Arts, which receives support from the Virginia General Assembly and the National Endowment for the Arts, a federal agency.

Fauquier Community Theatre is an Equal Opportunity Employer/Program. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.
