Cast List Announcement: Rumors

The secret is out! Congratulations to this incredible cast of 10 performers who will be starring in Fauquier Community Theatre’s Rumors, by Neil Simon!

The show runs weekends from January 22 through February 7, 2027 — tickets available now at fct.ludus.com. Don’t miss this hysterical, fast-paced production of cover-ups and escalating chaos!

Meet the Cast

  • Chris Gorman: Hannah McAfee
  • Ken Gorman: Erin Allen
  • Claire Ganz: Theano Mavroidis
  • Lenny Ganz: Larry Finkel
  • Ernie Cusack: Bob Thompson
  • Cookie Cusack: Dawn Campbell
  • Glenn Cooper: Val Kuperman
  • Cassie Cooper: Elizabeth Steimel
  • Officer Welch: Laura Gibson
  • Officer Pudney: Anthony Fuller

Directed by Marty Kelly
Produced by Sam Wong

Vint Hill Theatre on the Green
4225 Aiken Drive, Warrenton
