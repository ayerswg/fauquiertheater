Cast and Production Team Announced for Miracle on 34th Street, the Play

FCT’s production of the full length play The Miracle on 34th Street, the Play is directed by Michelle Matthews and by special arrangement with THE DRAMATIC PUBLISHING COMPANY of Woodstock, Illinois.” The play is adapted by Mountain Community Theater from the novel by Valentine Davies and based upon the Twentieth Century Fox motion picture Miracle on 34th Street.
