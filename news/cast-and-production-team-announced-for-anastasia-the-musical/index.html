Cast and Production Team Announced for Anastasia: The Musical

Thank you to all who auditioned!

Anastasia: The Musical will take place at Fauquier Community Theatre on Fridays and weekends from September 18 – October 4, 2026. Tickets ($18 – $24) are on sale now at fctstage.thundertix.com/. All tickets are for reserved seating; choose your favorite seating choices today! With a dynamic cast and production team, this musical is sure to become a favorite at Fauquier Community Theatre!

Congratulations to all!

Director: Camden Gillespie
Producer: Maggie Swan
Assistant Director/ Dramaturg: Lily VanBrocklin
Music Director: Nick Villacorte
Choreographer: Abby Chaumont

Synopsis: Her past is a mystery. Her future is an adventure. Anastasia: The Musical is the spectacular new musical about discovering who you are and defining who you’re meant to be.

From Tony winners Terrence McNally, Stephen Flaherty and Lynn Ahrens, creators of such Broadway classics as Ragtime and Once On This Island, this dazzling show transports its audience from the twilight of the Russian Empire to the euphoria of Paris in the 1920s, as a brave young woman sets out to discover the mystery of her past.

Pursued by a ruthless Soviet officer determined to silence her, Anya enlists the aid of a dashing con man and a lovable ex-aristocrat. Together, they embark on an epic adventure to help her find home, love and family. Book by Terrence McNally, Music by Stephen Flaherty, Lyrics by Lynn Flaherty. Inspired by the Twentieth Century Fox Motion Pictures by special arrangement with Buena Vista Theatrical and Concord Theatricals. From the play by Marcelle Maurette as adapted by Guy Bolton.
