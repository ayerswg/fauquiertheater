Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about tickets, performances, and getting involved.

Tickets & Box Office

When is the Box Office open?

The box office generally opens 45 minutes to 1 hour before curtain/showtime. Advance ordering is available anytime through our online ticketing system.

Where do I pick up tickets if I ordered online ahead of time?

Tickets can be printed from the email confirmation sent to you at the time of purchase. They are also available at Will Call during performances.

Where is Will Call?

Will Call is located inside the theatre in the lobby, at the desk across from the bathrooms.

What is a Season Subscriber?

Season subscribers receive discounted tickets for all regular season productions, with the benefit of purchasing five shows and receiving the sixth free.

How do I order a Season Subscription?

You can purchase through the online ticketing system, by mail, or by email at info@fauquiertheater.org.

As a Season Subscriber, how do I reserve my tickets?

Two ways: apply your unique coupon codes online or email your ticket requests.

Performances

May I video or photograph a performance?

The use of recording equipment and cameras (with or without flash) during performances is prohibited without prior approval.

Is FCT handicap accessible?

Yes. Wheelchair-accessible seats include B11, 13, 15, 17 and B12, 14, 16, 18, and accessible bathrooms are available. Auxiliary aids and services are available upon request to individuals with disabilities.

How do I know the recommended age appropriateness of a show?

Performances are generally kid-friendly unless indicated by a PG-13 rating on our website.

Playbill

How can my business get a spot in the playbill?

Become a business sponsor to gain recognition in the playbill. Contact FCT at info@fauquiertheater.org.

Acting & Volunteering

How can I act in a show?

All auditions are open for anyone to attend. Visit the auditions page for preparation details.

How can I volunteer?

Email info@fauquiertheater.org to help with the box office, or visit the get involved page for backstage, lighting, and sound opportunities.

About FCT

When is the new season of shows announced?

Each year a new season is announced around the month of March, prior to the season opener in the fall.

When are the Summer Youth show and camp announced?

Announcements appear on the website between January and March.

Is FCT a non-profit?

Yes, we are a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. All donations are tax-deductible.
