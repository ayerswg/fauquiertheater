Auditions

All auditions are open for anyone to attend. We try our best to make the experience fun and a time to meet new people.

Recent & Upcoming Auditions

Anastasia: The Musical

Held June 12 & 13, 2026

Auditions for our September 2026 production were held Friday, June 12 and Saturday, June 13, 2026, 6–10 pm. Directed by Camden Gillespie, produced by Maggie Swan. The cast and production team have been announced — see the news.

Calendar Girls

Held December 18 & 19, 2025

Auditions were held at the Vint Hill Theater on the Green, casting 9 women and 4 men, ages 18 and over. English accents were used, with assistance available, and there is no nudity in the production.

Tales of the Artisan

Held October 22, 2025

Auditions for the world premiere musical were held at the Vint Hill Theater on the Green for ages 15 and up.

New auditions are announced here, in the Spotlight newsletter, and on our Facebook page. Pre-registration through an online audition form is typically required — each announcement includes its own registration link.

Good to Know

  • All auditions are open — no experience with FCT is required, and newcomers are always welcome.
  • Auditions are held at the Vint Hill Theater on the Green, 4225 Aiken Drive, Warrenton, VA.
  • Adult cast members (18 and over) are required to hold a current FCT membership; youth cast memberships are $20. See membership details.
  • Not ready for the stage? There are plenty of backstage and front-of-house roles too.

Questions? Email info@fauquiertheater.org.
